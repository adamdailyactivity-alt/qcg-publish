01 · Origins · why COMPASS became QCG

The journey began in June 2026 with learning the computer and operating system from first principles—Terminal, chronology, human–machine interaction and control over local tools. It widened into Project COMPASS, then the QCG architecture: systems thinking, cybernetics and strategic independence grounded in practical evidence.

Existing Macs, cloud providers, AI services and applications can be used deliberately while clear off-ramps and recovery are developed. Sovereignty is not the claim that all third parties have already disappeared.

Distinct strategic destination

A governed Base Camp first; a human-readable Museum next; then longer-term independent infrastructure and systems engineering.

04 · Evidence architecture · what counts as proof

QCG maintains a strict distinction between original communication evidence, interpretive summaries and canonical admission. A clean website, screenshot or report cannot silently become original evidence or an authoritative record.

Working artefacts are versioned and checked against manifests. Provenance, timestamp, source, observation versus assertion, uncertainty and decision authority are treated as first-class fields. The operational discipline is one action → one proof → next action.

Current limitation: complete post-export verbatim preservation and some capture/export mismatches remain unresolved.

05 · Sovereign Centre · constitutional layering

The Sovereign Centre is the outer architectural boundary around the established Constitutional Centre. It should make the system coherent to a human, without moving every object into one folder or creating competing masters.

Its governed functions are authority, register, working, custody, ingress, Atlas/Museum, operations and recovery. Originals remain separately preserved; evidence and working derivatives retain distinct statuses. Active services stay in place until dependencies and rollback are proven.

Current state: a proposed coherent view and verified working records exist; whole-estate migration and admission have not been authorised.

Register 001 is a versioned, WORKING · UNADMITTED reference index. Six files matched their recorded SHA-256 manifest in repeat checks. MASTER: none admitted. Ownership, recovery and causal relationships remain to be proved.

07 · The QCG Atlas · six navigation questions

The Atlas is the human-readable map connecting records, provenance, relationships, uncertainty and authority, online and offline.

  1. What do we have?
  2. What do we know?
  3. What is true now?
  4. Has it changed?
  5. Are we protected?
  6. Where are we going?

The Atlas should allow someone unfamiliar with the system to find an original and understand the evidence behind a decision. A working Atlas concept and experimental readers exist; a fully accepted Atlas does not yet exist.

08 · Custody · preserve originals and versions

Historical freezes, conversation captures, source-labelled imports, encrypted archive candidates and manifests establish a growing custody practice. Recent consolidation measured 4,344 regular-file entries in a seven-group ingress collection; that count is neither a unique-content total nor evidence of canonical admission.

Two historical encrypted archive files with matching filenames and sizes produced different SHA-256 ciphertext fingerprints. They are preserved as distinct objects pending recovery and underlying-content verification.

Rule: preserve previous versions and error history. No destructive deduplication by name or size.

09 · Security · governance under load

Two live demonstration services and their networking behaviour were investigated. A Mac-only interim security boundary was selected, and a versioned pre-change working record passed independent structure, permission, JSON and manifest checks.

Critical distinction: a preservation record is not successful containment. Service hardening, access controls and rollback remain gated and subject to a separate approval. No hidden execution or automatic changes.

Detailed network identifiers and local paths are intentionally omitted from this partner-facing account.

10 · Recovery · independent or not proven

Backup candidates, local copies, Time Machine snapshots and encrypted custody artefacts have been observed; a limited independent one-file recovery receipt exists. But complete independently tested restoration, remote custody and key recovery remain unproven.

Base Camp needs bounded, repeatable recovery demonstrations; a real off-ramp; emergency stop and revocation provisions; and explicitly recorded failures. One physical directory is not a sovereign backup architecture.

11 · Crown Jewels · identity and signing

The actual Crown Jewels—irreplaceable evidence, knowledge, relationships and control points—must be identified by evidence, not intuition. Ownership, custody, relationship strength, signing authority and permission boundaries are still unresolved in important areas.

Design the human signing and admission authority before generating authoritative keys or granting machine action. Geolocation, where useful, remains optional, permissioned, timestamped, provenance-backed and privacy-controlled.

12 · Q¹ · Q² · Q³ continuity

Q¹ Connected: controlled conventional operation with explicit dependencies. Q² Offline digital: locally usable and independently recoverable information without internet. Q³ Physical: essential instructions, governance, maps and records remain intelligible without a computer or power.

The QCG Museum is intended to preserve the original human-readable trail and its proven relationships. End-of-January Museum acceptance remains a target, not an achieved state.
